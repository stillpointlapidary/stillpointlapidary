Why It Works

Your nervous system isn't broken. It's stuck.

Most people don't realize how much their baseline state shapes their life. The nervous system controls how you respond to stress, rest, and recovery — and most of us are running a constant low-level stress state without realizing it.

When that shifts, things that once felt difficult start to feel natural. Clarity, calm, the ability to respond instead of react.

Every modality at Still Point works directly with the nervous system — gently nudging it out of high-alert states and back toward regulation. Not forcing change. Allowing it.
