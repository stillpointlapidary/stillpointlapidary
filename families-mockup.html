Crystal Families
Why a jasper is not just a jasper — and why families matter
Crystal families are defined by mineral structure and chemistry, not appearance. Two stones that look nothing alike can share a family — and two stones with the same name can belong to entirely different ones. Understanding families helps you predict care requirements, energetic tendencies, and why certain stones work the way they do.
Why Families Share Energetic Tendencies
It's not coincidence that most Quartzes amplify, most Garnets energize, and most Calcites work with emotional flow. The mineral structure determines how a stone interacts with light, electricity, and subtle energy. Silicon dioxide (Quartz) is piezoelectric — it generates charge under pressure, which may be why so many Quartzes feel activating. Understanding the family gives you a head start on understanding any new stone within it.
Practical implication: If you receive an unfamiliar stone and it's in the Quartz family, you already know it will likely be durable (7+ Mohs), work well with amplification, and respond well to moonlight cleansing. The family is a shortcut.

Photo Brief — Big Seven
7 photos, same setup for all
All 7 photos should feel like a set: same surface, same light, same treatment. The tile grid will feel cohesive even before you have photos for every family.
Setup
  • 📐 Square crop (same as Stone of the Day)
  • 🧺 White marble or cream linen surface
  • 💡 Soft natural window light — no flash
  • 📏 One stone per shot, centered with breathing room
  • 🚫 No labels, no props, no jewelry
Processing
  • 📱 iPhone → square crop in Photos
  • ✨ Encyclopedia Photo Brightening preset
  • 🌐 CloudConvert → WEBP, 800px, q85
  • 💾 Name: fam-quartz.webp etc.
  • ☁️ Upload to Supabase storage
