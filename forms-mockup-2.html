Crystals 101

Crystal Forms

A form is the way a stone appears as a collectible piece — naturally grown, cut, polished, carved, or shaped. The form you choose changes how you work with a stone, how you carry it, and how it fits into your space.

Tumble
Polished, softened, accessible
Machine-polished until smooth. The most versatile and widely available form — easy to carry, hold, and work with daily.
Palm Stone
Made for touch, somatic, grounding
Shaped to fit the palm — flat, smooth, slightly rounded. Ideal for nervous system work, anxiety, and meditation.
Worry Stone
Repetitive touch, anxiety, presence
Flat oval with a thumb indentation. The physical repetition of rubbing grounds attention in the body. Keep one in a pocket.
Heart
Emotional, receptive, heart-centered
The shape itself is an intention. Used in emotional healing, self-love, and relationship work. Comforting to hold.
