## 8. Research Sequence

Research the cohort horizontally in this order:

1. identity, mineralogy, formation, treatment, locality, and collector-market research
2. metaphysical consensus and structured associations
3. care, related stones, pairings, use cases, and curiosity notes
4. normalization and evidence audit

Do not begin MD drafting before all four passes are complete.

## 11. Gate 0 Decision

A cohort is ready when:

- IDs, names, and slugs are confirmed
- Collection Tier is confirmed
- Material Type is confirmed or explicitly marked Pending Research
- chakra and Energetic Role inputs are confirmed or appropriately routed to research
- navigation status is known
- image status is known
- all exception flags are visible
- no unresolved Blocking item remains
- the production-data snapshot is recorded
