Use only the sources and rules below unless Christie explicitly approves an addition, removal, exception, or tier change.

- *The Crystal Bible Vol. 3* (Judy Hall)

### Tier A

Use as the primary online consensus core.

- Crystal Vaults
- Crystal Council
- Moonrise Crystals
- Crystals.com
- Crystal Almanac

### Tier B

Use as supporting sources only.

- Energy Muse
- Healing Crystals

Tier B may support an established consensus, but it may not independently establish, override, or resolve a disputed major claim.

### Planet Field — Sourcing Rule

Planet is a Tier A consensus field. A+ books do not systematically assign planetary correspondences.

Rules:
- Require support from at least two approved Tier A sources
- Conflicts between sources must be flagged
- A+ confirmation is not required for this field

## 3. Geological and Technical Source Hierarchy

### Core Geological Sources

Use these as the primary sources for mineral identity, physical properties, formation, and locality research:

1. Mindat
2. GIA, when the material is covered
3. Handbook of Mineralogy

### Specialty Verification Sources

Use these according to the specific question being verified:

- **IMA-CNMNC** — official mineral names, accepted species status, nomenclature, classification disputes, and questionable trade names
- **RRUFF** — technical identity, chemistry, Raman, XRD, IR, and crystallographic verification
- **USGS MRDS** — locality, deposit, occurrence, and mining-district verification
- **Walter Schumann, _Gemstones of the World_, 5th edition** — standard gemological context

Specialty sources support targeted verification. They do not replace the core geological hierarchy for a general mineral profile.

Retailer pages may be consulted only for clearly labeled market examples when specifically authorized. They may not establish mineralogical facts or metaphysical consensus.

---

## 5. Consensus and Conflict Rules

- Do not invent or reassign source tiers.
- Do not use unlisted metaphysical sources without Christie’s approval.
- Tier A+ and Tier A form the metaphysical consensus core.
- Tier B may support consensus but may not override stronger sources.
- Non-approved sources do not count toward source minimums.
- Geological claims must follow the geological and technical hierarchy.
- Official naming and classification questions must use IMA-CNMNC when applicable.
- Treatment, identity, or trade-name claims must be verified with the strongest relevant technical source available.
- Conflicts between reputable sources must be documented rather than silently resolved.
- When consensus is weak, disputed, or absent, reduce the strength of the public claim or omit it.
- Do not mark source hierarchy PASS when an unapproved source remains in the counted research set.

---

## 6. Source Recording Rules

- Record exact sources in internal research notes.
- Do not name sources in public encyclopedia copy unless editorially necessary.
- Distinguish geological fact, metaphysical consensus, and editorial synthesis.
- Record source disagreements, treatment uncertainty, and identity ambiguity.
- Preserve rejected or excluded claims in the internal research record when they materially affected editorial decisions.

---

## 7. Change Control

Any source addition, removal, tier change, or exception requires:

1. Christie’s explicit approval
2. an update to this document
3. a version change
4. review of any active cohort or draft that relied on the previous hierarchy

This document remains authoritative until a newer approved version replaces it.
