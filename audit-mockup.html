Crystals 101 — Section Headers & Body Text
Working With Crystals
Section 1 of 8
There's no single right method. Crystal work doesn't require ritual, belief, or expertise. What it requires is attention — and a willingness to notice what shifts when you bring something physical into your environment or your hands.
Crystals don't think. What intention-setting does is prime your own mind to notice relevant feelings, patterns, and shifts.
The stone becomes a physical anchor for something you're already doing internally. That's not a small thing — it's the same mechanism behind journaling, prayer, or any other physical practice that supports inner work.
